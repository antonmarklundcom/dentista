## What this data says

1. **Prótesis dental is the money keyword:** 1 300 searches and the highest bid in the export
   (9,59–31,17 kr). The site has no service page for it yet, only the "dientes postizos" guide.
   Build `/servicios/protesis-dental/` next.
2. **"consultorios odontologicos" is the biggest term (1 900),** plus "clinica odontología"
   (880) and "consultorios dentales" (170). These people are looking for a clinic, not a
   treatment. Build a page such as `/consultorios-odontologicos/` aimed at them.
3. **Self-ligating brackets are a cluster of ~1 100/month:** autoligables 480, autoligado 320,
   auto ligado 320, plus zafiro 210 + 90. That is worth its own page (or a strong section on
   `/servicios/brackets/`).
4. **"blanqueo dental" gets 1 000 searches with a high bid (15,38 kr).** Add it as a
   synonym on the blanqueamiento page (title and an H2).
5. **Toothache is about 4 000 searches/month in total, all informational.** Main terms:
   remedios caseros 1 300 + 320, para dolor de dientes 390, dolor de muela 320, and
   medication queries. Expand the `dolor-de-muelas` guide to cover them, without doses.
   Every visit that lands there is a potential urgent lead.
6. **Competitor clinics are searched by name:** integra odontologia 1 000, dental guarani
   1 000, una odontologia 320, dental universitaria 320, dental mas 260, innovaciones
   dentales 210. The site can't rank for these, but they are the biggest clinics in the
   market and therefore the first partners to pitch.
7. **Dentist-side (B2B) search is basically zero (all 10/month).** Partners have to come from
   outreach (WhatsApp, visits, Instagram), not SEO. The partner page is a sales tool to send
   people to, not a traffic page.
8. **Missing from this export:** implantes dentales, ortodoncia, endodoncia / tratamiento de
   conducto, corona dental, diseño de sonrisa, all on 4, carillas de resina. Re-check them.
   The implantes page currently has `volume => null`.
